SF Desk for Windows
===================

You need:
  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. SF-Desk-windows.zip is for Intel and AMD PCs, SF-Desk-windows-arm64.zip
    for ARM PCs (Snapdragon, e.g. Surface Pro or Copilot+ PCs). Settings > System > About shows which.
  - For your meetings in Time Entry, one of:
      - Microsoft 365: works with the new Outlook. Pick "Sign in to read your Microsoft 365 calendar"
        in Time Entry's calendar list, open the page it shows and enter the code. Your organization may
        need to approve "Microsoft Graph Command Line Tools" first; SF Desk says so if it does.
      - Classic Outlook signed in to your SailPoint mailbox (the "New Outlook" toggle switched off).
      - Salesforce events, which need nothing else.
  - Microsoft Edge (it comes with Windows). SF Desk opens in an Edge window of its own.

To install:
  1. Right-click the zip > Extract All..., then open the extracted folder.
  2. Double-click "Install SF Desk". If Windows SmartScreen warns about it, click More info > Run anyway.
  3. SF Desk starts. The first time it offers to download the Salesforce CLI (no admin rights
     needed) and opens your browser to sign in to Salesforce with SSO.
  4. Click Connect, then open Time Entry and pick your calendar at the top. With classic Outlook,
     Outlook may ask whether to allow access to its data; choose Allow.

SF Desk is then in the Start menu and on the Desktop. Closing its window keeps it running; the SF Desk
icon by the clock reopens it, opens its settings and log, or quits it.

To update, run the new "Install SF Desk" the same way. Your settings and data are kept.
To uninstall, quit SF Desk and delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SF Desk, %LOCALAPPDATA%\SF Desk and the
two SF Desk shortcuts.
