SF Desk for Mac
===============

Before you start
- Add your SailPoint Exchange account in System Settings > Internet Accounts (Add Account >
  Microsoft Exchange, sign in with your SailPoint email, turn on Calendars), and check that this
  week's meetings show in Calendar.app. SF Desk's Time Entry tab reads them from there.
- You need a Salesforce login to sailpoint.my.salesforce.com (your usual SSO login).

Install
1. Double-click "Install SF Desk.command".
   If macOS says it can't verify the developer: click Done, open System Settings > Privacy &
   Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to "Install SF Desk.command", then confirm.
   (Or right-click the file > Open > Open.)
2. A Terminal window walks you through the rest: it installs SF Desk into your home folder
   (~/SF-Desk), the Salesforce CLI if you don't have it, and signs you in to Salesforce in your
   browser.
3. When macOS asks whether Terminal may access your calendars, choose Allow Full Access.

SF Desk opens in your browser at http://localhost:3000. Click Connect.

Every day
Double-click "Start SF Desk" on your Desktop. Keep its Terminal window open while you use SF Desk;
close the window to stop it.

Updating
Unzip the new download and run "Install SF Desk.command" again. Your settings, login and local data
are kept.

Uninstalling
Delete the SF-Desk folder in your home folder and "Start SF Desk" on your Desktop.

Problems? See INSTALL.md in ~/SF-Desk, "Troubleshooting".
